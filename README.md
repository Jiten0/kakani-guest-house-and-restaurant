# Kakani Guest House & Restaurant

A responsive, static website for Kakani Guest House and Restaurant in Kakani, Nepal.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this directory with any static HTTP server.

## Contact

The site has no online booking or messaging form. Visitors can call the public business number, visit the Facebook page, or open directions in Google Maps. These links and the address are based on the business's public Maps listing.

## Site interactions

- Stay and dining tabs switch the featured visit details.
- The business photo slideshow supports previous/next controls, direct slide selection, and arrow-key navigation.
- The mobile navigation collapses into an accessible menu.
- The light/dark theme toggle follows the visitor's system preference initially and remembers their selection.
- The embedded map links to directions in Google Maps.

## Photo sources

The slideshow uses the Maps listing cover image (used with permission) and two photos from the business's public Facebook photo gallery. Other visitor-contributed Maps photos are not included.
